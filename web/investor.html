The road to 10 August 2027

Every client must be signed before the April primaries. After August, winners keep paying for constituency-office tools.

    The product

    Real IEBC data for every county, constituency, ward and polling station. Open the live demo and pick any seat in Kenya.

    Why Kampein wins

    There is at least one other platform chasing 2027 campaigns. The market is big enough for several; we win on how we reach and serve aspirants.

    Distribution through trusted voices

    Political media partners, starting with Herman Manyora, put Kampein in front of aspirants of every party, paid only on results.

    Built for devolution

    Five seats, 47 counties, 1,450 wards. No presidential race, so no camp to be tied to, and every product decision is about county politics.

    People behind the software

    Every campaign gets a named support champion, daily support and round-the-clock cover on polling day, plus paid in-person training for field teams (KES 20,000 a day).

    Fits how Kenyan campaigns run

    M-Pesa for every payment, Telegram for agents on cheap phones, two-clerk tallying on the results form for each seat, and prices aspirants can see upfront.
