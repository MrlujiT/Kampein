Field team and targets

Know who is working, where, and against what target

Your candidate, campaign manager, ward coordinators and agents each see only their own patch. Set your expected turnout and vote share, and Kampein turns it into the votes needed in every ward and the weekly field work to get there.

Door-to-door visits, meetings and rallies are logged as they happen, and every ward is ranked daily, so you see where the campaign is strong and where it has gone quiet.

Money

Every shilling budgeted, approved and traced

Build the budget line by line: posters, matatu hire, agent stipends. Every payment request draws on a line, nobody approves their own request, and nothing is marked paid without its M-Pesa reference.

Payments go only to registered team members and vendors. Nothing is ever deleted: every change leaves a record of who and when.

Election day · rolling out December

No figure counts until two clerks agree

On polling day your agent photographs the signed results form for your seat (35A for MP, 36A for MCA, 37A for Governor, 38A for Senator, 39A for Woman Rep) and sends it in. Two clerks type the figures separately; only matching entries join your tally.

It is your campaign's internal tally and your evidence if a result is disputed. IEBC alone declares results.
